It is the only terracotta object in the film, together with the key word *Lebensfreude*.

Font: Playfair Display only. Colours: cream `#f7f1e8`, ink `#2f2922`, muted `#857c6e`,
line `#ece2d1`, white cards, accent `#b8542e`; gold `#bb9540` only in the logo stars.
Camera: continuous push-in of ×1.04 per 3 s scene, never resetting (1.000 → 1.217).
All texts are taken verbatim from www.ristorante-pizzeria-italia.com.
