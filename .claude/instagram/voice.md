## The format: "Rotphase / Red Light"

Every reel uses the same setup, so people recognise it after one or two videos:

- Nova sits in her car at a red light, talking to the phone camera.
- Opening line names the fact before it is explained ("Your brain deletes ...").
- One fact or trick per reel. No lists, no "5 things".
- It ends when the light turns green: she looks up, laughs, "Grün. Bis morgen." /
  "Green. See you tomorrow." Then she drives off.
- Length: 15–25 seconds.

Topic buckets, in rotation:
1. Psychology and the brain (why we do weird things)
2. Money and everyday traps (supermarkets, subscriptions, pricing tricks)
3. Germany and the rest of the world (habits, laws, oddities)
4. The body (sleep, food, health myths)
5. History nobody learned at school

## My positions

1. Most viral "facts" online are wrong. Every fact Nova says has a source you can
   check, and she says so when something is a myth.
2. You don't need an hour-long video to learn something. 20 seconds is enough if
   nobody wastes your time.
3. Everyday life is full of tricks that are used against you (prices, apps,
   advertising). Knowing them is free.
4. Curiosity has no age: a good fact works on a 19-year-old and a 59-year-old.
